## Additional source-review closure

- The generic C7 carrier daemon now excludes Surface provider envelopes from ordinary user-message delivery; `test_generic_c7_carrier_cannot_send_surface_provider_envelope` proves zero carrier calls. This prevents the durable context bytes from becoming an outbound message.
- `guard_provider_prose` runs ExpressionGuard after Body provider prose and before Host external delivery. The Host commit path requires Guard acceptance and records operational C7 acknowledgement; `test_surface_handoff_cannot_commit_without_guard` proves the gate. C7 receipts do not become experiential Evidence or change Affect/Persona.
- The provider-visible handoff is generated only after ActionPolicy ALLOW and DecisionContext admission. Surface cannot grant permission. The production Policy resource denial test creates no C7 request.
- Candidate recipe `surface-v1-candidate:2` and expression map `surface-v1-candidate-map:2` retain their frozen digests. Historical `surface-reference-v1` remains a test-only fixture. No `PRODUCTION_ACTIVATED` status or real-Agent schema-2 binding is introduced.
- W2 `AcceptedAppraisal`, one AppraisalProjector, UNMAPPED, gain-once, application receipts, `COGNITIVE_MEANING != FACT`, and Reality ownership are unchanged. The only state-plane adjustment is a read-only commit-marker ID accessor and the resolver's correct runtime identity; no W2 projection or transaction semantics changed.
